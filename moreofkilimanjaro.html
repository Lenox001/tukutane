Things to Do in Kilimanjaro

Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa’s highest peak, is a dream destination for climbers and adventure seekers. With multiple routes like Marangu, Machame, and Lemosho, trekkers experience breathtaking landscapes, from lush rainforests to icy summits.

The park surrounding Kilimanjaro is home to a rich diversity of wildlife, including elephants, leopards, and unique plant species. Hiking through the foothills reveals stunning waterfalls, scenic trails, and an unforgettable connection with nature.

The Chagga people, who live around Kilimanjaro, have a deep cultural heritage. Visitors can explore their villages, experience traditional food, and participate in coffee tours to learn about the region’s rich coffee-growing history.
