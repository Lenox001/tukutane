Discover Samburu National Reserve

Unlike other parks in Kenya, Samburu is home to rare species known as the "Samburu Special Five": the Grevy’s zebra, reticulated giraffe, Somali ostrich, beisa oryx, and gerenuk. The Ewaso Ng’iro River supports a thriving ecosystem, attracting large elephant herds and predators like lions and leopards.

Samburu’s landscape is breathtaking, with rugged hills, riverine forests, and acacia-dotted plains. The local Samburu people, closely related to the Maasai, add to the region’s cultural richness, offering visitors a glimpse into their vibrant traditions and nomadic lifestyle.

A safari in Samburu promises a less crowded, more intimate experience compared to other parks. Whether spotting leopards along the riverbanks or witnessing elephants crossing the Ewaso Ng’iro, the reserve offers unforgettable wildlife encounters. Camel safaris and bush walks add to the adventure.
