Exploring Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya is Africa’s second-highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Climbers can tackle peaks such as Batian, Nelion, and Point Lenana, each offering unique challenges and rewarding views. Trekking through the diverse ecosystems—from bamboo forests to alpine meadows—is an unforgettable experience.

The slopes of Mount Kenya are home to unique wildlife, including elephants, buffalos, and colobus monkeys. The forests and moorlands provide breathtaking scenery with waterfalls, lakes, and glaciers that make every step of the journey worthwhile.

Mount Kenya offers some of the best camping spots in East Africa. The clear night skies at high altitudes make it a perfect place for stargazing, with views of the Milky Way and shooting stars lighting up the night.
